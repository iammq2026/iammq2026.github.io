Our Philosophical Commitment

"Every species is an irreplaceable thread in the living tapestry of earth."

Founded in 2012 by wildlife veterinarians and field conservationists, VANA was established to bypass corporate greenwashing bureaucracy. We allocate 98% of all funds directly to boots-on-the-ground animal rescue, forest land trust purchase, and indigenous ranger employment.
